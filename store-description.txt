Know what your machine is doing at a glance.

Plasmetrics is a compact system monitor for the Plasma 6 panel. Each metric shows as a label, a slim vertical bar and a value, so you can read your system's state without opening anything. Click it for a popup with history graphs, load average, top processes and uptime.

Metrics: CPU, memory, network, temperature, battery (level, wattage, voltage) and disk.

Make it yours
- Show only the metrics you care about, and drag them into your preferred order
- Choose bar, history chart, percentage, or chart plus percentage for each one
- CPU can also show one little square per core, colored green, orange or red by load
- Choose the battery, network interface, temperature sensor and disk to watch, including wireless mouse and keyboard batteries
- Pick colors from a fixed palette, set your font and sizes, switch °C/°F
- Vertical or horizontal panel labels

Light and simple. No background services. It reads /proc and /sys every couple of seconds and nothing else.
